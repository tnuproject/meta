# Code of Conduct

Everyone taking part in META is expected to make it a good place to work.

## Expected

- Be respectful. Disagree about ideas without attacking people.
- Give and accept technical criticism in good faith.
- Assume others have good reasons; ask before judging.
- Admit mistakes and help fix them.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, threats, insults, or sexual attention.
- Discrimination or demeaning comments about who someone is.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Scope

This applies to the repository, issues, pull requests, reviews and any other place
where someone represents META.

## Reporting

Report problems privately to a maintainer listed in
[GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md). Reports are kept confidential. If the report is about
a maintainer, contact a different one.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, and may warn, suspend or exclude
participants, in proportion to what happened. Decisions can be appealed once to the
maintainers as a group.
